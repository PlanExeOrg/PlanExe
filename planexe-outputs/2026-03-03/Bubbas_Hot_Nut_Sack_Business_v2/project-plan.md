## Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategies


### Key Risks

- Regulatory fragmentation across 12+ states (e.g., CA Prop 65, NY 'spicy' claims) causing delays (4–8 weeks) and re-labeling costs ($15K–$30K/state)
- Kraft sleeve moisture degradation in high-humidity Sun Belt climates (>80% RH), risking spoilage >4% and margin erosion
- Brand name rejection by conservative retailers (e.g., 40% Whole Foods buyer rejection), threatening 15%+ revenue
- Capsaicin exposure incidents causing OSHA fines ($15K/incident) and reputational harm
- Co-packer chestnut spoilage (8–12%) due to cold-chain failures, disrupting production and increasing COGS
- Heat Club UGC challenges triggering TikTok policy violations or PR crises

### Diverse Risks

- Operational risk: Labor turnover (60%+) in Sun Belt affecting quality and timelines
- Business risk: National brand imitation (e.g., KIND, Clif Bar) within 6–9 months
- Financial risk: Margin compression from cost spikes extending breakeven by 4–7 months
- Supply chain risk: Price volatility (±22%) in heirloom nuts disrupting hybrid sourcing model

### Mitigation Plans

- Reallocate $25K from marketing to regulatory contingency; require state-by-state compliance matrix before packaging finalization; add 'regulatory gate' to timeline; use modular inner-liner inserts for low-cost state variants
- Conduct 60-day accelerated humidity test at 80% RH; add food-grade compostable moisture-barrier coating (+$0.03/unit); pilot with Houston co-packer; switch to standard pouches in Southeast if spoilage >4%
- Pause full launch until securing 1 anchor retail partner; leverage Heat Club pre-orders to prove demand; rebrand to 'Bubba's Nut Sack' for conservative channels; co-create 'Heat Safety Pledge' with American Spice Trade Association
- Equip all staff with N95, nitrile gloves, and 100+ CFM ventilation; conduct monthly OSHA emergency drills with sodium thiosulfate stock; install capsaicin monitors (0.01 mg/m³ alarm); require third-party SQF audit
- Dual-source all nuts; lock in 12-month forward contracts with price caps; invest in shelf-life extension (MAP + rapid-turn inventory); secure regional backup co-packer within 300 miles
- Embed 'Responsible Heat' framework in Heat Club terms (age-gated sign-up, mandatory disclaimers, influencer pre-approval); auto-flag TikTok videos with 'nuclear/extreme' claims; require 3-minute online safety module for challenge access
