## Primary Decisions
The vital few decisions that have the most impact.

Critical levers focus on foundational trade-offs: Production Scalability Model (capital vs. control), Supply Chain Localization Strategy (cost vs. quality consistency), and Market Access Strategy (local reach vs. regional distribution). High importance levers address operational efficiency and growth: Production Facility Configuration (capital efficiency vs. flexibility), Operational Cost Structure (logistics costs vs. customer convenience). Medium importance levers focus on differentiation and sustainability: Product Portfolio Strategy (product complexity vs. market differentiation), Customer Acquisition Approach (marketing spend vs. organic growth potential), Brand Positioning (market differentiation vs. production costs). Low importance levers are tactical or redundant: Community Integration Approach, Revenue Stream Mix, and Production Capacity Planning.
