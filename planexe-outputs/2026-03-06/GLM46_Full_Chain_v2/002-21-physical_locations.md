This plan implies one or more physical locations.

## Requirements for physical locations

- rural Connecticut location
- zoning permits for commercial roasting operations
- access to utilities (electricity, water)
- space for modular roasting system and storage
- proximity to local farmers for bean sourcing where possible
- reasonable access for customer pickup or delivery logistics

## Location 1
USA

Rural Connecticut (e.g., Litchfield County)

Industrial or light commercial zone in towns like Sharon, Washington, or Torrington

**Rationale**: Litchfield County offers a rural setting with growing demand for artisanal products. Light industrial zones allow for roasting operations while maintaining proximity to local farms and scenic appeal.

## Location 2
USA

Hartford County, Connecticut

Shared industrial space or warehouse district in towns like Simsbury or Avon

**Rationale**: Simsbury and Avon provide access to a larger regional market while maintaining rural characteristics. Shared industrial spaces reduce upfront capital costs.

## Location 3
USA

New Haven County, Connecticut

Mixed-use commercial zone in towns like Woodbridge or Bethany

**Rationale**: These areas offer a balance between rural charm and accessibility to urban markets. Mixed-use zoning may allow for tasting room events alongside production.

## Location Summary
The plan requires a physical location in rural Connecticut suitable for small-scale coffee roasting operations. The suggested sites prioritize access to utilities, appropriate zoning, proximity to local farms, and logistical feasibility for distribution or customer pickup.
