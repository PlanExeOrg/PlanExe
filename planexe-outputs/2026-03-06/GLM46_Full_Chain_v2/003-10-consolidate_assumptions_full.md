# Identify Risks


## Risk 1 - Regulatory & Permitting
Failure to obtain necessary zoning permits and environmental clearances for commercial roasting operations in rural Connecticut, potentially leading to operational delays or shutdowns.

**Impact:** Delays of 2-4 months in project timeline, potential extra costs of $5,000-$10,000 for legal consultations and permit applications.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Severity:** High

**Action:** Proactive engagement with local town planning departments, obtaining detailed zoning maps for potential locations, and preparing comprehensive environmental impact assessments to demonstrate compliance with local regulations.

## Risk 2 - Technical
Inadequate technical expertise to manage multiple roasting systems effectively, leading to inconsistent coffee quality, increased operational downtime, and potential damage to equipment.

**Impact:** Inconsistent coffee quality affecting customer satisfaction and brand reputation, potential operational downtime of 1-2 weeks during equipment troubleshooting, and increased maintenance costs by $3,000-$5,000 annually for specialized technical support.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Severity:** High

**Action:** Invest in comprehensive training programs for roasting equipment operation and maintenance, consider hiring experienced coffee roasters or technical specialists to oversee operations, and establish regular equipment maintenance schedules with certified technicians.

## Risk 3 - Financial
Unexpected fluctuations in coffee bean prices or energy costs, leading to increased operational expenses and reduced profit margins.

**Impact:** Increased operating costs by 15-20% annually, reduced gross profit margins from expected 40% to approximately 30%, and potential impact on business viability if cost increases are not offset by price adjustments or increased sales volume.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Severity:** High

**Action:** Develop comprehensive cost management strategies including regular market price monitoring for coffee beans and energy costs, implementing hedging strategies or bulk purchasing agreements to mitigate price volatility, and maintaining flexible budgeting processes that allow for adjustments in response to changing cost factors.

## Risk 4 - Supply Chain
Seasonal availability constraints of coffee beans sourced from local farmers in rural Connecticut, leading to potential supply shortages during peak roasting seasons.

**Impact:** Supply disruptions affecting 20-30% of total coffee bean requirements during peak roasting periods, potential delays in production schedules and customer orders, and increased costs due to emergency sourcing from non-local suppliers.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Severity:** High

**Action:** Develop diversified sourcing strategies that include partnerships with multiple local farmers to mitigate seasonal availability risks, implementing advanced inventory management systems to monitor coffee bean stock levels and trigger replenishment orders in advance of peak demand periods, and maintaining contingency plans for emergency sourcing from non-local suppliers if local availability constraints become critical during peak roasting seasons.

## Risk 5 - Operational
Inadequate logistics planning for delivery or pickup of coffee products in rural Connecticut, leading to customer dissatisfaction due to long delivery times or difficulty accessing pickup points.

**Impact:** Customer satisfaction scores declining by 15-20% due to logistics issues, potential loss of repeat customers and negative word-of-mouth referrals affecting future customer acquisition rates.

**Likelihood:** Medium

**Severity:** High

**Action:** Develop comprehensive logistics planning strategies including establishing multiple local pickup points at partner businesses in rural Connecticut to improve customer access and reduce delivery costs, implementing flexible delivery options such as local pickup or scheduled delivery slots to accommodate customer preferences and logistical constraints in rural areas, and maintaining regular communication with logistics partners to monitor delivery performance and address any issues promptly to ensure customer satisfaction.

## Risk summary
The 2-3 most critical risks are regulatory permitting delays, technical expertise gaps in managing multiple roasting systems, and financial risks from unexpected fluctuations in coffee bean prices or energy costs. These risks could significantly jeopardize the project's success if not properly managed.
