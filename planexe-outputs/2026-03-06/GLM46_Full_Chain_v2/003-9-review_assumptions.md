## Issue 1 - Regulatory Compliance & Permitting Delays
The project assumes immediate compliance with all local zoning and environmental regulations, without accounting for potential delays in permit acquisition or regulatory approval processes.

**Recommendation:** Implement a proactive regulatory compliance strategy by engaging with local town planning departments early in the project lifecycle, obtaining detailed zoning maps and understanding of permitting timelines for commercial roasting operations. Prepare comprehensive environmental impact assessments to demonstrate compliance with local regulations and accelerate the permitting process.

**Sensitivity:** A delay in obtaining necessary zoning permits and environmental clearances for commercial roasting operations in rural Connecticut, potentially leading to operational delays or shutdowns. Impact: Delays of 2-4 months in project timeline, potential extra costs of $5,000-$10, 000 for legal consultations and permit applications. Likelihood: Medium Severity: High

## Review conclusion
The project faces significant regulatory and permitting risks that could critically impact the timeline, budget, and overall success of establishing a small-scale coffee roasting business in rural Connecticut. Proactive engagement with local authorities, thorough preparation of compliance documentation, and contingency planning for potential permitting delays are critical actions to mitigate these risks.
