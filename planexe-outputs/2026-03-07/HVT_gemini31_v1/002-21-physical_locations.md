This plan implies one or more physical locations.

## Requirements for physical locations

- Large outdoor or semi-enclosed area for drone flight
- Zoning for recreational/entertainment use
- Safety clearance for drone operations (FAA/local aviation compliance)
- Infrastructure for modular urban-mimicry obstacles
- Proximity to urban centers for customer acquisition
- Power and connectivity for haptic systems and drone charging

## Location 1
USA

Industrial Districts, Austin, Texas

Repurposed warehouse districts or large vacant industrial lots

**Rationale**: Austin offers a tech-forward demographic and a regulatory environment that is generally receptive to drone innovation, making it ideal for the 'Pragmatic Foundation' strategy.

## Location 2
USA

Greater Los Angeles Area, California

Large-scale outdoor recreational parks or converted film studio backlots

**Rationale**: The high density of corporate headquarters and entertainment-seeking consumers supports the 'tiered membership' and 'corporate team-building' revenue models.

## Location 3
USA

Research Triangle Park, North Carolina

Purpose-built modular arenas on commercial land

**Rationale**: Proximity to engineering talent and tech hubs provides a strong base for maintaining the semi-autonomous drone fleet and haptic feedback systems.

## Location Summary
The plan requires physical sites that balance safety, regulatory compliance, and immersion. The suggested locations in Austin, Los Angeles, and the Research Triangle provide the necessary infrastructure for modular urban-mimicry arenas while ensuring access to the target demographics required for the 'Pragmatic Foundation' business model.
