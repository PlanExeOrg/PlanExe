## Currencies

- **USD:** The project is based entirely within the United States (Austin, Los Angeles, Research Triangle Park), making the United States Dollar the standard and only required currency.

**Primary currency:** USD

**Currency strategy:** As the project is confined to the United States, the local currency (USD) will be used for all transactions, including capital expenditures for infrastructure, drone hardware procurement, insurance premiums, and operational revenue. No international currency risk management is required.
