# Identify Risks
# Risk 1: Regulatory and Permitting

- Context: FAA Part 107, Part 89, and local noise/privacy ordinances.
- Impact: Fleet grounding, permit denial, 3-6 month delays, and legal fines.
- Likelihood: High. Severity: High.
- Action: Engage aviation counsel, implement hard-coded geofencing, and maintain FSDO communication.

# Risk 2: Technical and Operational

- Context: Signal interference, latency, and collisions in urban-mimicry arenas.
- Impact: Hardware damage, player injury, system downtime, and $2,000-$5,000 per collision.
- Likelihood: High. Severity: Medium.
- Action: Use redundant non-GPS positioning (UWB/optical flow) and conduct rigorous stress testing.

# Risk 3: Financial and Market

- Context: High capital expenditure and potential for high customer acquisition costs.
- Impact: Cash flow insolvency within 12-18 months; loss of $250k-$500k per site.
- Likelihood: Medium. Severity: High.
- Action: Prioritize high-margin corporate events and maintain lean, scalable infrastructure.

# Risk 4: Supply Chain

- Context: Reliance on specialized, ruggedized drone and haptic components.
- Impact: 2-4 week fleet downtime, missed bookings, and brand damage.
- Likelihood: Medium. Severity: Medium.
- Action: Maintain 20% spare parts buffer and diversify vendor sourcing.

# Risk 5: Social and Safety

- Context: Community backlash regarding noise, privacy, and military-style drone perception.
- Impact: PR crisis, negative media, and potential forced relocation.
- Likelihood: Medium. Severity: Medium.
- Action: Use low-noise propellers, conduct community outreach, and enforce strict perimeter containment.

# Risk Summary

- Critical threats: Regulatory compliance, operational safety, and financial sustainability.
- Safety: Failure in semi-autonomous logic or physical barriers risks injury, litigation, and shutdown.
- Financial: High capital intensity requires high throughput and diversified revenue streams to withstand market volatility.

## Issue 1: Insurance Market Viability

- Assumption: Insurance is a manageable cost.
- Risk: Novel, high-risk autonomous drone activities often face exclusions in standard liability policies.
- Recommendation: Conduct an Insurance Feasibility Study and engage a specialty broker before site leasing. If premiums exceed 15-20% of revenue, implement higher safety-tier hardware.
- Sensitivity: Premiums 3x higher than standard rates (5% of revenue) reduce ROI by 10-15%, risking project viability.

## Issue 2: Latency and Interference

- Assumption: Local Area Networks (LAN) suffice for connectivity.
- Risk: Urban RF noise causes signal drops and fly-aways, leading to safety incidents.
- Recommendation: Perform professional RF site surveys. Utilize dedicated, licensed, or private 5G/CBRS networks instead of standard Wi-Fi.
- Sensitivity: A 100ms latency increase raises collision rates by 20-30%, adding $15,000–$30,000 in annual maintenance costs per site.

## Issue 3: CAC vs. Churn

- Assumption: Tiered memberships ensure recurring revenue.
- Risk: High novelty decay leads to significant churn after initial visits.
- Recommendation: Budget for quarterly content refreshes (game modes, maps, software). Allocate 10% of annual revenue to R&D to maintain a 40%+ repeat-customer rate.
- Sensitivity: If repeat-customer rates drop below 25%, CLV falls by 35%, extending the break-even point from 18 to 30+ months.

## Review Conclusion
The project relies on best-case assumptions. Addressing insurability, RF stability, and novelty decay through proactive surveys, specialty underwriting, and R&D budgeting is required to transition from a high-risk venture to a scalable model.
