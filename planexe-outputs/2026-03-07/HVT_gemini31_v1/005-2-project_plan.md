**Goal Statement:** Launch a commercial HVT drone combat simulation venue within 9 months, achieving operational sustainability through a tiered membership model and high-immersion, semi-autonomous drone technology.

## SMART Criteria

- **Specific:** Establish a fully operational, safety-compliant HVT arena featuring modular urban-mimicry obstacles, a 20-unit drone fleet with semi-autonomous engagement logic, and integrated haptic feedback systems.
- **Measurable:** Success is measured by securing specialty liability insurance, obtaining FSDO operational approval, maintaining a 40%+ repeat-customer rate, and achieving break-even within 18 months of launch.
- **Achievable:** The project is achievable by following the 'Pragmatic Foundation' strategy, which balances high-tech immersion with manageable capital expenditure and phased regulatory compliance.
- **Relevant:** This project disrupts the recreational combat market by providing a unique, high-stakes simulation experience that differentiates itself from traditional paintball or VR gaming.
- **Time-bound:** The project must be completed within a 9-month timeline, encompassing site acquisition, construction, and certification.

## Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategies


### Key Risks

- Regulatory shutdown due to FAA/FSDO non-compliance
- Catastrophic injury leading to litigation and insurance cancellation
- RF signal interference causing mid-air collisions
- High customer churn after initial novelty wears off

### Diverse Risks

- Operational downtime due to hardware fragility
- Community backlash regarding noise and privacy
- Financial insolvency from high capital expenditure

### Mitigation Plans

- Implement hard-coded geofencing and independent hardware-level kill switches on all units
- Engage specialty insurance brokers early to ensure policy viability
- Use private 5G/CBRS networks to eliminate latency and interference issues
- Budget 10% of annual revenue for R&D and content refreshes to maintain repeat-customer rates
- Install low-noise propellers and conduct community outreach to maintain zoning compliance
