### 1. Regulatory Compliance and Safety Audit Monitoring
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Safety Case Documentation
  - FSDO Correspondence Log
  - Compliance Checklist
  - Incident/Near-Miss Reporting Database

**Frequency:** Bi-weekly

**Responsible Role:** Ethics, Safety & Compliance Committee (ESCC)

**Adaptation Process:** ESCC reviews audit findings; if non-compliance is detected, they issue a mandatory corrective action plan to the OMT. If safety is compromised, the ESCC exercises its veto power to halt operations.

**Adaptation Trigger:** New regulatory requirement identified, safety incident reported, or audit finding indicates non-compliance with FAA/FSDO standards.

### 2. Financial Sustainability and Budget Variance Analysis
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Financial Reporting Dashboard
  - Budget Variance Spreadsheet
  - Capital Expenditure Tracker

**Frequency:** Monthly

**Responsible Role:** Project Steering Committee (PSC)

**Adaptation Process:** PSC reviews burn rate against milestones; if variance exceeds thresholds, they reallocate funds, adjust the project scope, or initiate a strategic pivot.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Capital expenditure exceeds $25,000 threshold or projected break-even date shifts by >1 month.

### 3. Operational Throughput and Customer Retention Monitoring
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - CRM/Membership Database
  - Operational Performance Metrics (KPIs)
  - Customer Feedback/Churn Analysis Report

**Frequency:** Weekly

**Responsible Role:** Operational Management Team (OMT)

**Adaptation Process:** OMT analyzes churn metrics; if repeat-customer rates fall below 40%, they adjust content, game modes, or marketing strategies to improve engagement.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Repeat-customer rate drops below 40% or customer churn exceeds the established quarterly threshold.

### 4. Technical Performance and Hardware Reliability Monitoring
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Maintenance Log
  - Fleet Availability Tracker
  - RF Network Stability Report

**Frequency:** Weekly

**Responsible Role:** Operational Management Team (OMT)

**Adaptation Process:** OMT reviews Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF); if hardware failure rates increase, they adjust maintenance schedules or initiate procurement of spare parts from the 20% buffer.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Hardware failure rate increases by >15% or RF latency exceeds safety-critical thresholds.
