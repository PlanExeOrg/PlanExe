
## Audit - Corruption Risks

- Favoritism in the selection of specialized drone hardware or haptic feedback vendors, potentially bypassing competitive bidding to favor personal contacts.
- Kickbacks or 'consulting fees' accepted by project personnel from insurance brokers in exchange for selecting high-premium, high-commission policies.
- Misuse of proprietary drone-AI engagement software or trade secrets by employees to launch competing recreational drone ventures.
- Conflicts of interest regarding the selection of site locations (e.g., Austin, LA, or RTP) where project leads may have undisclosed real estate holdings or financial stakes in the chosen properties.
- Trading favors with local municipal zoning officials to expedite permits or bypass noise/privacy ordinance compliance checks.

## Audit - Misallocation Risks

- Diversion of the $450,000 seed capital into unauthorized R&D projects or personal expenses under the guise of 'hardware procurement'.
- Inefficient allocation of the 20-unit drone fleet, where high-end industrial drones are used for low-value testing instead of revenue-generating sessions.
- Double-spending on redundant RF infrastructure (e.g., purchasing both standard Wi-Fi and private 5G/CBRS hardware) due to poor procurement oversight.
- Misreporting of 'operational downtime' to hide the fact that equipment is being used for unauthorized private events or side-hustles.
- Unauthorized use of the 10% annual R&D budget for non-project-related technology upgrades or luxury office improvements.

## Audit - Procedures

- Quarterly financial audits of all capital expenditures, specifically verifying the $450,000 seed capital usage against invoices for drone hardware and arena construction.
- Bi-annual technical audits of the drone fleet and safety systems (proximity sensors, kill switches) to ensure they match the documented safety case submitted to the FSDO.
- Monthly reconciliation of revenue from tiered memberships and corporate events against booking logs to detect potential skimming or under-reporting.
- Annual compliance review of FAA Part 107/Part 89 certifications and local zoning permits to ensure no lapses in regulatory standing.
- Periodic 'blind' safety audits where an independent party tests the responsiveness of hardware-level kill switches and geofencing parameters during simulated flight.

## Audit - Transparency Measures

- Implementation of a public-facing project dashboard displaying real-time safety incident rates, noise decibel levels, and compliance status with local ordinances.
- Publication of quarterly 'Community Impact Reports' detailing noise mitigation efforts, battery recycling metrics, and summaries of neighborhood council meeting minutes.
- Establishment of a formal, anonymous whistleblower mechanism for staff to report safety violations, hardware tampering, or suspected conflicts of interest.
- Documented, public-access selection criteria for all major vendor contracts (e.g., drone manufacturers, insurance providers) to ensure fair competition.
- Regularly updated 'Project Health' log available to investors, detailing the status of the 9-month timeline, current burn rate, and progress toward the 18-month break-even goal.
