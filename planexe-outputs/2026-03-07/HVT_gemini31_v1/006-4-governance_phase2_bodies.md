### 1. Project Steering Committee (PSC)

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Required to provide high-level strategic direction, manage the $450,000 seed capital, and oversee the high-stakes regulatory and financial risks inherent in a novel drone-combat venture.

**Responsibilities:**

- Approve major capital expenditures exceeding $25,000.
- Oversee long-term financial sustainability and break-even progress.
- Manage high-level relationships with investors and regulatory bodies.
- Finalize strategic pivots (e.g., franchise model vs. direct operation).

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Draft and sign the Project Charter.
- Appoint an independent external advisor for regulatory/safety oversight.
- Establish the financial reporting dashboard.

**Membership:**

- Project Lead
- Lead Investor Representative
- Independent Regulatory/Aviation Consultant (External)

**Decision Rights:** Authority over strategic direction, budget thresholds above $25,000, and final approval of site selection.

**Decision Mechanism:** Consensus-based; in the event of a tie, the Independent Regulatory Consultant holds the casting vote to ensure safety and compliance priority.

**Meeting Cadence:** Monthly

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Financial burn rate and budget variance analysis
- Regulatory compliance status (FAA/FSDO)
- Strategic risk register review
- Site acquisition and construction milestones

**Escalation Path:** Board of Directors or Primary Investors for issues involving project insolvency or major regulatory shutdown.
### 2. Operational Management Team (OMT)

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Necessary to manage the day-to-day execution of the 9-month launch timeline, drone fleet maintenance, and site operations.

**Responsibilities:**

- Execute daily site operations and staff management.
- Manage drone fleet maintenance and hardware procurement.
- Monitor RF network stability and latency.
- Implement safety protocols and emergency response procedures.

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Define standard operating procedures (SOPs) for flight operations.
- Establish the maintenance log and inventory tracking system.
- Set up the internal communication and incident reporting channel.

**Membership:**

- Site Manager
- Drone Technicians (2)
- Safety/Range Officer

**Decision Rights:** Operational decisions below $25,000; day-to-day scheduling and maintenance priorities.

**Decision Mechanism:** Majority vote; Site Manager holds the tie-breaker.

**Meeting Cadence:** Weekly

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Fleet availability and maintenance status
- Safety incident reports and near-miss analysis
- Staffing and training updates
- Customer feedback and churn metrics

**Escalation Path:** Project Steering Committee for any operational issue impacting safety compliance or exceeding the $25,000 budget threshold.
### 3. Ethics, Safety & Compliance Committee (ESCC)

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Critical for managing the high-risk nature of human-drone interaction, ensuring FAA/FSDO compliance, and mitigating community backlash.

**Responsibilities:**

- Conduct independent safety audits of hardware and geofencing.
- Oversee GDPR and privacy compliance regarding player data.
- Manage community relations and noise mitigation reporting.
- Review and approve all safety-critical software updates.

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Develop the 'Safety Case' documentation for FSDO submission.
- Establish the anonymous whistleblower mechanism.
- Create the Community Impact Report template.

**Membership:**

- Safety/Range Officer
- External Legal Counsel (Aviation/Liability)
- Community Liaison (External/Independent)

**Decision Rights:** Veto power over any flight operations or hardware configurations deemed unsafe or non-compliant with regulations.

**Decision Mechanism:** Unanimous consent required for safety-critical changes; if no consensus, the External Legal Counsel makes the final determination.

**Meeting Cadence:** Bi-weekly

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Review of recent safety incidents or near-misses
- Regulatory audit findings (FAA/FSDO)
- Community feedback and noise complaint logs
- Insurance policy compliance review

**Escalation Path:** Project Steering Committee for immediate cessation of operations due to safety violations.
