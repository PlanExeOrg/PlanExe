**Budget Request Exceeding $25,000**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Consensus-based vote by PSC members; Independent Regulatory Consultant holds the tie-breaking vote.
Rationale: The Operational Management Team (OMT) is limited to expenditures below $25,000; larger amounts require strategic oversight to protect seed capital.
Negative Consequences: Uncontrolled burn rate leading to project insolvency or inability to fund critical infrastructure phases.

**Critical Safety Violation or Regulatory Non-Compliance**
Escalation Level: Ethics, Safety & Compliance Committee (ESCC)
Approval Process: Unanimous consent required; External Legal Counsel makes the final determination if no consensus is reached.
Rationale: Safety and regulatory compliance are the project's 'license to operate'; violations require immediate, expert-led intervention.
Negative Consequences: Permanent loss of FAA/FSDO operating permits, catastrophic injury, or total project shutdown.

**Operational Deadlock on Safety-Critical Hardware Configuration**
Escalation Level: Ethics, Safety & Compliance Committee (ESCC)
Approval Process: Formal review of safety audit findings followed by a binding veto or approval decision by the ESCC.
Rationale: The ESCC holds veto power over any configuration deemed unsafe, ensuring that operational speed does not compromise safety standards.
Negative Consequences: Increased risk of mid-air collisions, player injury, and potential litigation.

**Strategic Pivot (e.g., Franchise Model vs. Direct Operation)**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Strategic review and consensus-based vote by the PSC, considering long-term financial sustainability.
Rationale: Major shifts in the business model fundamentally alter the project's risk profile and capital requirements, exceeding the scope of operational management.
Negative Consequences: Misalignment with investor expectations, wasted capital on non-scalable models, and failure to achieve break-even targets.

**Project Insolvency or Major Regulatory Shutdown Threat**
Escalation Level: Board of Directors or Primary Investors
Approval Process: Executive review and formal resolution by the Board/Investors.
Rationale: When the project faces existential threats that the PSC cannot resolve, the ultimate owners must decide whether to continue funding or liquidate.
Negative Consequences: Total loss of investment, legal liability for stakeholders, and reputational damage.
