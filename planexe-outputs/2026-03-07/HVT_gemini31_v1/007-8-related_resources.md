## Suggestion 2 - BattleBots (Live Event Operations)

A professional combat robotics league where custom-built, high-speed machines engage in full-contact combat within a reinforced, bulletproof-glass arena. It is the pinnacle of high-speed, high-risk autonomous/remote-controlled combat.

### Success Metrics

Maintained a perfect safety record for spectators over decades of operation.
Established industry-standard 'kill switch' and containment protocols for high-energy robotics.
