# Omissions

## 1. On-site Medical/First Aid Support

Given the high-stakes, physical nature of the simulation and the potential for high-speed drone collisions or player trips/falls, the current team lacks a dedicated resource for immediate medical response.

**Recommendation**:
Designate one of the existing staff members (e.g., the Safety/Range Officer) to hold a current Wilderness First Responder or advanced First Aid/CPR certification, and maintain a fully stocked trauma kit on-site.

## 2. Inventory & Supply Chain Coordinator

The project relies on a 20% spare parts buffer and specialized hardware. Without a dedicated role to track inventory levels and manage vendor lead times, the operation risks significant downtime.

**Recommendation**:
Assign the 'Inventory Management' responsibility to the Drone Systems & Robotics Engineer as a formal part of their role, utilizing a simple automated tracking system to trigger reorders before stock hits critical levels.

# Potential Improvements

## 1. Role Overlap: Safety/Range Officer vs. Site Manager

There is a potential conflict between the Site Manager's goal of high throughput and the Safety/Range Officer's goal of risk mitigation, which could lead to friction during peak operational hours.

**Recommendation**:
Clearly define the 'Safety/Range Officer' as having absolute authority to halt operations (the 'Stop-Work Authority'), ensuring this power is explicitly documented in the operational handbook to prevent pressure from the Site Manager to prioritize speed over safety.

## 2. Simplification of Part-Time Roles

The project currently lists several distinct roles that could be consolidated to reduce administrative overhead and improve communication flow.

**Recommendation**:
Merge the 'Community & Public Relations Coordinator' and 'Risk Management & Insurance Liaison' into a single 'External Relations & Compliance' role, as both require similar skills in stakeholder management and regulatory documentation.

## 3. Cross-Training for Technical Resilience

With only two to three Drone Systems & Robotics Engineers, the project is vulnerable if a key technical staff member is absent during a critical failure.

**Recommendation**:
Implement a mandatory cross-training program where the Site Operations Manager is trained on basic drone 'hot-swapping' and emergency hardware-level kill switch resets to ensure the facility can remain functional during minor technical hiccups.
