## 1. Insurance Feasibility and Liability Underwriting

Insurance is the primary 'license to operate.' Without it, the project faces catastrophic financial and legal risk.

### Data to Collect

- Quotes from specialty aviation insurance brokers for autonomous drone-human interaction.
- Exclusion clauses in standard recreational liability policies.
- Safety Management System (SMS) documentation requirements for underwriters.

### Simulation Steps

- Use risk modeling software (e.g., @RISK or Crystal Ball) to simulate potential financial impact of insurance premiums on ROI.
- Create a 'worst-case' financial scenario model in Excel to test the 15% revenue threshold.

### Expert Validation Steps

- Consult with an aviation liability attorney to review policy language.
- Engage specialty brokers (e.g., Global Aerospace, Allianz) to obtain a binding insurance quote.

### Responsible Parties

- Project Manager
- Risk Management & Insurance Liaison

### Assumptions

- **High:** Specialty insurance for autonomous drone-human interaction is available at a cost below 15% of revenue.

### SMART Validation Objective

Secure a binding insurance quote or a formal letter of insurability from a specialty broker within 90 days.

### Notes

- If premiums exceed 15%, the project must pivot to tethered or simulated-only drone interactions.


## 2. RF Spectrum and Latency Validation

Latency and interference are critical failure points for autonomous drone safety and player immersion.

### Data to Collect

- RF spectrum analysis reports for target urban sites (Austin, LA, RTP).
- Latency measurements for private 5G/CBRS network configurations.
- Signal propagation data in the presence of metallic urban-mimicry obstacles.

### Simulation Steps

- Perform 3D ray-tracing propagation simulations using software like Wireless InSite or Atoll.
- Model latency impact on collision rates using custom Python scripts to simulate drone flight paths.

### Expert Validation Steps

- Consult with an RF Systems Engineer to validate the network topology.
- Conduct on-site spectrum analysis with a certified technician.

### Responsible Parties

- Technical Lead
- Network & Telemetry Specialist

### Assumptions

- **High:** Private 5G/CBRS networks can maintain sub-20ms latency in urban environments.

### SMART Validation Objective

Achieve a consistent sub-20ms latency in a simulated urban-mimicry environment within 60 days.

### Notes

- Failure to meet latency targets will result in high collision rates and poor user experience.


## 3. Regulatory Compliance and Safety Case

Regulatory non-compliance leads to immediate shutdown and loss of all invested capital.

### Data to Collect

- FAA Part 107/Part 89 compliance requirements.
- Local noise ordinance decibel limits for target cities.
- Safety Case documentation for 'Operation Over Human Beings'.

### Simulation Steps

- Use FAA's DroneZone portal to simulate the waiver application process.
- Model noise propagation using software like SoundPLAN to ensure compliance with local ordinances.

### Expert Validation Steps

- Consult with an Aviation Regulatory & Compliance Officer.
- Schedule a pre-application meeting with the local Flight Standards District Office (FSDO).

### Responsible Parties

- Aviation Regulatory & Compliance Officer
- Project Manager

### Assumptions

- **High:** FSDO will approve the 'Pragmatic Foundation' safety case for autonomous operations.

### SMART Validation Objective

Obtain a formal FSDO response or preliminary approval for the operational safety case within 120 days.

### Notes

- The project must pivot to 'pilot-in-command supervised' if full autonomy is rejected.

## Summary

The HVT project is a high-stakes venture requiring immediate validation of its 'license to operate' (insurance and regulatory compliance) and its 'technical backbone' (RF stability). The most critical next step is to pause all capital expenditure until a binding insurance quote is secured and a pre-application meeting with the FSDO is conducted. These actions mitigate the highest-sensitivity risks that could lead to project insolvency or legal shutdown.
