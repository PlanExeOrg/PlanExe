# HVT: The Future of Tactical Drone Combat

## Project Overview
HVT is a first-of-its-kind, **immersive drone combat simulation** that bridges the gap between high-stakes tactical gaming and physical robotics. By integrating modular urban-mimicry environments with **semi-autonomous swarm technology** and haptic feedback, we are creating a new category of high-intensity entertainment. This platform transforms participants into the protagonists of their own tactical missions, offering a **scalable, premium experience** that redefines the recreational combat market.

## Risks and Mitigation Strategies
We prioritize a **safety-first architecture** to address operational concerns and regulatory scrutiny. Our mitigation strategy includes:

- Implementation of **hard-coded geofencing** to maintain strict operational boundaries.
- Integration of **hardware-level kill switches** for immediate control.
- Proactive engagement with the FAA and FSDO to ensure **full regulatory compliance** prior to launch.
