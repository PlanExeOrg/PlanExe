## Question Answer Pair 2
**Question**: What are the primary regulatory and insurance risks associated with operating autonomous drone combat simulations?
**Answer**: The project faces significant 'license to operate' risks. FAA regulations (Part 107/89) do not currently treat fully autonomous flight over human participants as a standard recreational activity, requiring complex safety cases and waivers. Simultaneously, standard liability insurance policies often exclude 'intentional contact' or 'autonomous flight,' making it difficult to secure coverage. Without these, the project faces immediate shutdown and catastrophic financial liability.
**Rationale**: This highlights the most critical 'showstopper' risks identified in the document, which are essential for understanding the project's high-stakes nature and the necessity of the proposed mitigation strategies.

## Question Answer Pair 6
**Question**: What is the 'novelty decay' risk, and how does it threaten the project's financial model?
**Answer**: Novelty decay refers to the risk that the HVT experience fails to sustain repeat interest after the initial visit. Because the project has high fixed costs and capital intensity, it is highly sensitive to customer churn. If the experience does not evolve rapidly, the break-even point could extend from 18 months to over 30 months, potentially leading to financial insolvency.
**Rationale**: This addresses a key business risk identified in the documentation, explaining why the project must invest in R&D and content refreshes to remain viable.

## Question Answer Pair 9
**Question**: What is the 'Safety Management System' (SMS) and why is it critical for the project's regulatory success?
**Answer**: An SMS is a formal, documented framework that outlines safety policies, risk management, and operational procedures. For the HVT project, it is critical because the FAA and FSDO require evidence of proactive safety management—not just reactive hardware kill switches—to approve operations involving autonomous flight over human participants. Without a robust SMS, the project is unlikely to receive the necessary waivers for operation.
**Rationale**: This clarifies the distinction between technical safety (hardware) and systemic safety (governance), which is a major point of contention in the expert reviews provided in the document.
