## Checklist

## 1. Violates Known Physics

*Does the project require a major, unpredictable discovery in fundamental science to succeed?*

**Level**: ✅ Low

**Justification**: Rated LOW because the plan relies on conventional, well-understood drone technology (Part 107/89 compliance) and standard recreational infrastructure (modular arenas), avoiding any reliance on FTL, anti-gravity, or other prohibited physics-breaking concepts.

**Mitigation**: None


## 2. No Real-World Proof

*Does success depend on a technology or system that has not been proven in real projects at this scale or in this domain?*

**Level**: 🛑 High

**Justification**: Rated HIGH because the plan combines autonomous drone-human interaction in public-facing urban arenas—a novel, high-risk system—without independent evidence at scale. The plan explicitly acknowledges this, noting that 'FAA regulations (Part 107/89) do not currently treat fully autonomous flight over human participants as a standard recreational activity,' creating an existential regulatory and liability failure mode.

**Mitigation**: Project Manager: Establish parallel validation tracks for (1) Regulatory/Safety (FSDO safety case/waiver) and (2) Insurance (binding specialty policy) with a hard NO-GO gate at 120 days. Owner: Project Manager / Binding Insurance & FSDO Approval / 120 days.


## 3. Buzzwords

*Does the plan use excessive buzzwords without evidence of knowledge?*

**Level**: 🛑 High

**Justification**: Rated HIGH because the plan relies on undefined strategic concepts like 'Pragmatic Foundation' and 'HVT experience' without a clear mechanism-of-action (inputs→process→customer value). The plan lacks a defined owner for the overarching strategy and fails to link 'strategic' concepts to measurable decision hooks, as evidenced by the 'Weakness' sections in the levers which admit the options 'fail to consider' critical impacts like customer acquisition costs and technical latency.

**Mitigation**: Project Manager: Draft a one-pager for each strategic lever defining the mechanism-of-action, success metrics, and decision hooks, to be finalized and approved by the leadership team within 30 days.


## 4. Underestimating Risks

*Does this plan grossly underestimate risks?*

**Level**: 🛑 High

**Justification**: Rated HIGH because the plan lacks a comprehensive risk register that maps second-order cascades, such as 'FSDO permit denial → loss of insurance → cash flow insolvency → project shutdown.' While individual risks are identified, the plan fails to explicitly model these interdependencies, leaving the project vulnerable to systemic failure modes like the 'Insolvency Trap' (FM1) and 'Regulatory Shutdown' (FM3).

**Mitigation**: Risk Management Lead: Develop a formal dependency map linking regulatory, financial, and operational risks, and implement a monthly review cadence to update the register, within 30 days.
