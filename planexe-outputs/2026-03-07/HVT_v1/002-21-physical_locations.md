This plan implies one or more physical locations.

## Requirements for physical locations

- FAA Part 107 compliant airspace availability
- Year-round operational capacity (indoor/controlled environment preferred)
- Commercial zoning permitting entertainment and drone operations
- Sufficient square footage for safety buffer zones and player movement corridors
- Accessibility for customers including parking and public transport links

## Location 1
USA

Atlanta, Georgia (Peachtree City Industrial Corridor)

Designated industrial warehouse district suitable for commercial drone testing zones

**Rationale**: Central US location with high accessibility and lower operational costs; the region supports year-round drone operations due to milder climate compared to northern states, reducing seasonal downtime risks.

## Location 2
USA

Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas (South Industrial Corridor)

Large format warehouse facilities near DFW International Airport non-controlled airspace zones

**Rationale**: Proximity to major aviation infrastructure facilitates FAA Part 107 compliance and regulatory support; high population density ensures strong customer acquisition for the target market.

## Location 3
USA

Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania (Allegheny County Technology Park)

Converted industrial space within a designated technology and innovation zone

**Rationale**: Strong local ecosystem for drone technology integration and testing; proximity to university research centers supports the Technology Integration Strategy and regulatory partnership development.

## Location Summary
The plan requires physical locations that support year-round commercial drone operations with FAA Part 107 compliance. The suggested sites in Atlanta, Dallas-Fort Worth, and Pittsburgh offer industrial warehouse space suitable for safety modifications, ensuring operational continuity regardless of weather while meeting zoning requirements for entertainment venues.
