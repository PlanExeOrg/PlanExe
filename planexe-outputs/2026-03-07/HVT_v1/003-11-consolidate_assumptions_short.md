# Identify Risks
## Risk 1 - Regulatory & Permitting

- Dallas-Fort Worth location near DFW International Airport risks airspace waivers and no-fly zones due to proximity or national security events.
- Impact: 2-4 month launch delay, fines up to $10,000 per violation, 5-10 days annual downtime during lockdowns, legal costs over $25,000.
- Likelihood: High
- Severity: High
- Action: Detailed airspace analysis before lease signing; rapid re-booking protocol for no-fly events; consider Atlanta or Pittsburgh as primary hubs.

## Risk 2 - Technical Safety & Liability

- Custom collision sensors and high-intensity play risk hardware failure, latency spikes, or collisions striking players/bystanders.
- Impact: Injury lawsuits/class actions; insurance premiums up 40-60%; operational shutdowns 2-8 weeks; maintenance costs eroding margins by 15%.
- Likelihood: Medium
- Severity: High
- Action: Redundant safety systems (netting/stop zones); strict player screening; weekly hardware stress tests; commercial liability insurance with drone clauses.

## Risk 3 - Financial & Operational Viability

- High CAPEX for warehouse modifications and sensor integration creates fragile model sensitive to customer acquisition costs and thin net margins (<10%).
- Impact: Cash flow deficits beyond 6-month runway; additional funding at unfavorable terms; investment overrun by 20-30% ($150k-$300k); break-even pushed to Year 2.
- Likelihood: High
- Severity: Medium
- Action: Phase modifications for partial revenue during fit-out; fixed-price contracts with contractors/suppliers; maintain separate 6-month operating cash reserve.

## Risk 4 - Cybersecurity & Data Privacy

- Real-time player tracking telemetry creates cyberattack targets compromising privacy and violating data protection regulations.
- Impact: Regulatory fines $50k+; reputational damage causing 30% booking drop; loss of corporate contracts; remediation costs $100k-$200k.
- Likelihood: Medium
- Severity: High
- Action: Penetration testing prior to launch; end-to-end encryption for telemetry data; clear privacy policies with explicit consent; incident response plan.

## Risk 5 - Market Perception & Branding

- Military themes in commercial venues risk community backlash, zoning complaints, or parental restrictions on youth participation.
- Impact: Customer acquisition 15-20% lower than projected; negative media coverage; regulatory scrutiny; marketing pivot costs $40k.
- Likelihood: Medium
- Severity: Medium
- Action: Community town halls pre-opening; dual-branding approach (tactical sport vs combat simulation); grievance channel for residents regarding noise/safety.

## Risk summary

- Critical risks include regulatory compliance near airports, technical safety liability, and cybersecurity vulnerabilities in tracking systems.
- Indoor venues mitigate weather risk but increase CAPEX exposure.
- Operational flexibility trades off against regulatory certainty; Part 107 licenses are vital but complicated by airport proximity.
- Mitigation requires redundant safety hardware, cybersecurity audits, and secondary location plan (Atlanta/Pittsburgh) to reduce airspace dependency.
