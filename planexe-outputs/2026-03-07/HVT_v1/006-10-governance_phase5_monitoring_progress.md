### 1. Regulatory Compliance & FAA Part 107 Licensing Tracking
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - FAA Licensing Application Tracker Spreadsheet
  - Compliance Checklist for Airspace Waivers
  - Risk Register Document

**Frequency:** Weekly during launch phase; Monthly post-launch

**Responsible Role:** Safety & Compliance Oversight Board / Safety Compliance Coordinator

**Adaptation Process:** Adjust venue selection priority (e.g., prioritize Atlanta over DFW) or extend timeline if delays exceed thresholds. PSC reviews for strategic location changes.

**Adaptation Trigger:** FAA application delay exceeds 3 months from baseline; Rejection of Part 107 license; Airspace waiver denial due to airport proximity.

### 2. Safety Incident & Hardware Reliability Monitoring
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Safety Incident Log System
  - Hardware Stress Test Reports
  - Physical Inspection Checklists (Netting/Stop Zones)

**Frequency:** Weekly during fit-out and testing; Daily during operations

**Responsible Role:** Venue Operations Managers / Safety Compliance Coordinator

**Adaptation Process:** Implement corrective actions for incidents (e.g., modify flight envelopes); Suspend operations if thresholds exceeded; Escalate to Safety Board.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Any reportable safety incident involving players/bystanders; Hardware failure rate exceeds 5% during stress tests; Insurance premium increase >40%.

### 3. Technology Performance & Cybersecurity Validation
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - System Uptime Dashboard
  - Latency Logs and Telemetry Metrics
  - Penetration Test Reports

**Frequency:** Weekly during testing; Continuous monitoring post-launch

**Responsible Role:** Technology & Innovation Advisory Group / CTO

**Adaptation Process:** Adjust encryption protocols if vulnerabilities found; Replace hardware suppliers if latency issues persist; Update security patches.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Sensor latency exceeds 150ms during gameplay; Security audit finding of high severity vulnerability; Data breach detected.

### 4. Financial Viability & Revenue Scalability Tracking
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Project Financial Dashboard (CAPEX/Revenue)
  - CRM for Corporate Contracts and Partnerships
  - Customer Retention Analytics Tool

**Frequency:** Monthly

**Responsible Role:** Project Steering Committee / CFO

**Adaptation Process:** Adjust marketing spend or pricing models; Reallocate funds from non-essential CAPEX to working capital if needed. PSC approves major pivots.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Monthly customer retention rate falls below 15%; CAPEX overrun exceeds 10% of budget; Break-even timeline pushed beyond Year 2 without justification.

### 5. Market Perception & Branding Alignment Review
**Monitoring Tools/Platforms:**

  - Community Feedback Survey Platform
  - Social Media Sentiment Analysis Tool
  - Marketing Conversion Metrics Dashboard

**Frequency:** Bi-weekly during launch; Monthly post-launch

**Responsible Role:** Ethics & Community Relations Committee / Marketing Director

**Adaptation Process:** Pivot branding narrative (e.g., more tactical sport focus); Adjust community engagement strategies based on town hall feedback. PSC approves rebranding.

**Adaptation Trigger:** Negative sentiment trend increases by >20%; Youth participation rates drop below projected levels due to backlash; Community complaints regarding noise/safety exceed threshold.
