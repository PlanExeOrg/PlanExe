Consistency Check: The PSC Independent Aviation Advisor holds a veto on safety/regulatory matters which creates potential conflict with the Safety & Compliance Oversight Board's external consultant who also validates regulatory compliance; clear hierarchy for final regulatory sign-off is needed to prevent deadlock.
3. Gap 1 (Whistleblowing): The framework lacks an explicit, anonymous whistleblower channel for staff to report safety or ethical breaches without fear of retaliation from leadership.

5. Gap 3 (Audit Ownership): Phase 1 Audit Procedures identify corruption risks but do not assign specific ownership within the governance bodies to execute quarterly procurement audits, leaving accountability ambiguous.

## Summary

The HVT governance framework establishes a robust multi-layered structure prioritizing regulatory compliance and safety through specialized boards, with clear escalation paths for critical risks. Key strengths include the integration of external aviation expertise and dedicated ethics oversight; however, effectiveness depends on resolving potential veto conflicts between bodies and defining granular delegation limits to prevent operational bottlenecks during high-pressure incidents.
