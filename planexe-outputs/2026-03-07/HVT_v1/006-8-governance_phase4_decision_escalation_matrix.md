**CAPEX Expenditure Requesting Approval Beyond PMO Authority**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Majority voting with independent aviation advisor veto on safety/regulatory matters.
Rationale: Exceeds financial authority limit of $100,000 per location for operational management and impacts strategic budget allocation.
Negative Consequences: Budget overrun, cash flow deficits, project viability risk.

**Imminent Regulatory Shutdown or Critical Safety Violation**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Strategic review meeting with immediate decision on continuation vs. relocation/legal action.
Rationale: Threatens legal existence and requires high-level strategic intervention beyond safety board suspension authority.
Negative Consequences: Legal penalty, project shutdown, reputational damage.

**Proposed Major Technology Pivot or Strategic Hardware Change**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Technology strategy review followed by PSC vote on viability and CAPEX allocation.
Rationale: Involves significant investment risk and changes core product safety/feature profile requiring strategic approval.
Negative Consequences: Loss of competitive advantage, increased liability, wasted capital expenditure.

**Critical Brand Rebranding Due to Community Backlash**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Ethics Committee recommendation reviewed by PSC for strategic alignment and revenue impact approval.
Rationale: Fundamental change to market positioning requires financial commitment beyond committee authority.
Negative Consequences: Market rejection, loss of IP value, reduced customer acquisition.

**Operational Deadlock Between Safety Compliance and Launch Schedule**
Escalation Level: Project Steering Committee (PSC)
Approval Process: Emergency arbitration session with CEO/Founder tie-breaker authority on launch date vs safety standards.
Rationale: Requires higher level decision to resolve conflict between operational continuity and regulatory compliance.
Negative Consequences: Safety liability, project delays, legal exposure from launching prematurely or losing market window.
