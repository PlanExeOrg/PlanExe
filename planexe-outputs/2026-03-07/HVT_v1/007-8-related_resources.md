## Suggestion 1 - Drone Racing League (DRL) - Commercial Drone Entertainment Venues

Founded in 2015 by Sebastian Thrun and Matt Roberts, DRL operates as the world's first professional drone racing league with commercial venues across multiple US states including Las Vegas, New York, Los Angeles, and international locations. The company secured FAA Part 107 waivers for public drone demonstrations, built permanent arena infrastructure with safety netting systems, achieved broadcast partnerships with ESPN and NBC, and raised over $65M in venture capital funding by 2020 before being acquired by the UFC in 2023. Operations require certified pilots, crowd control protocols, and comprehensive liability insurance for high-speed autonomous drone flights.
