
## Audit - Corruption Risks

- Procurement kickbacks from drone or custom sensor suppliers inflating costs within the $500,000 CAPEX budget per location.
- Expedited FAA Part 107 licensing or zoning permits through improper regulatory relationships bypassing standard safety checks.
- Contractor collusion on warehouse modifications (HVAC/netting) to inflate invoices while delivering substandard safety infrastructure.
- Bribery in franchise licensing approvals allowing regional operators to launch without meeting required safety standards.
- Misuse of player telemetry data by staff for personal gain or selling data to third parties violating GDPR-like protocols.

## Audit - Misallocation Risks

- Diverting hardware budget ($500,000 CAPEX) away from critical safety modifications like ballistic netting due to supply chain delays.
- Using the $3,000 employee training fund for general retail staff instead of the required 40% certified flight instructors.
- Depleting the $50,000/year insurance premium contingency fund on operational expenses before securing binding quotes.
- Inefficient allocation of warehouse CAPEX resulting in utilization rates below break-even thresholds due to seasonal weather risks.
- Diverting funds allocated for AES-256 encryption cybersecurity infrastructure to marketing or cash flow needs.

## Audit - Procedures

- Quarterly procurement audits comparing vendor invoices against market rates for drones and custom collision sensors.
- Unannounced spot checks of warehouse safety modifications (HVAC, netting) before operational launch to verify permit compliance.
- Monthly tracking audits of FAA Part 107 license application status and renewal timelines across all three locations.
- Annual third-party penetration testing validation for telemetry data encryption (AES-256) prior to public sessions.
- Quarterly variance analysis comparing actual insurance premiums against the $50,000 contingency budget.

## Audit - Transparency Measures

- Public dashboard publishing monthly safety incident reports and maintenance logs on the venue website.
- Publication of vendor selection criteria for drone suppliers and contractors to ensure fair procurement practices.
- Clear display of privacy policies regarding player telemetry data at registration points and online portals.
- Public disclosure of franchise licensing terms and eligibility criteria for potential regional operators.
- Publishing minutes from biannual community town halls addressing noise and safety concerns before full launch.
