### 1. Project Steering Committee (PSC)

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Critical for high-stakes commercial venture with $500k CAPEX per location, multi-location operations across three states, and regulatory/commercial viability risks. Requires strategic oversight to balance innovation with operational stability while managing significant capital expenditure and market expansion ambitions.

**Responsibilities:**

- Approve all CAPEX expenditures exceeding $100,000 per location
- Review and approve quarterly financial performance against break-even targets
- Make strategic decisions on franchise model implementation and regional expansion
- Oversee regulatory compliance strategy including FAA Part 107 licensing timeline risks
- Approve binding insurance quotes and contingency fund allocations above $50,000 annually
- Resolve conflicts between operational flexibility and regulatory requirements

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Finalize Terms of Reference defining decision thresholds and escalation paths
- Appoint independent external member with aviation industry expertise
- Establish quarterly meeting schedule aligned with financial reporting cycles
- Define clear separation between PSC strategic decisions and PMO operational decisions

**Membership:**

- CEO/Founder (Chair)
- Chief Financial Officer
- Operations Director
- Independent External Advisor - Aviation Industry Expert (Non-voting advisory role with veto on safety/regulatory matters)
- Lead Investor Representative

**Decision Rights:** Strategic decisions including CAPEX approvals above $100,000 per location, franchise model implementation, regulatory strategy approval, insurance contingency fund allocations above $50,000 annually, and expansion to new geographic markets

**Decision Mechanism:** Majority voting with independent aviation advisor having veto authority on safety/regulatory matters. Tie-breaker resolved by CEO/Founder in consultation with investor representative.

**Meeting Cadence:** Monthly during project launch phase (first 12 months), quarterly thereafter once operations stabilize

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Quarterly financial performance review and CAPEX utilization analysis
- FAA Part 107 licensing status updates across all three locations
- Insurance premium contingency fund allocation decisions
- Franchise model readiness assessment and regional expansion timing
- Risk register update with regulatory, safety, and market perception risks

**Escalation Path:** Issues exceeding PSC authority on strategic direction or unresolved conflicts escalate to Board of Directors. Safety/regulatory veto matters by independent advisor require immediate FAA compliance review.
### 2. Operational Management Office (PMO)

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Essential for day-to-day execution management across three geographically dispersed locations (Atlanta, Dallas-Fort Worth, Pittsburgh). Required to coordinate warehouse modifications, hardware procurement, staff training, and safety system implementation while managing CAPEX constraints and operational continuity risks.

**Responsibilities:**

- Manage daily operations across all three venue locations
- Oversee warehouse modification progress and HVAC safety compliance
- Coordinate custom collision sensor integration with quadcopter systems
- Track staff certification training (40% flight instructors, 60% retail staff)
- Monitor cybersecurity infrastructure implementation including AES-256 encryption
- Manage vendor relationships for drone suppliers and contractors within budget constraints

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Establish standardized operational procedures across all three locations
- Implement weekly reporting dashboard for CAPEX tracking per location
- Create incident logging system for safety events and maintenance activities
- Define clear escalation criteria to PSC based on financial thresholds

**Membership:**

- Project Manager (PMO Lead)
- Venue Operations Managers (one per location - Atlanta, Dallas-Fort Worth, Pittsburgh)
- Procurement Specialist
- Safety Compliance Coordinator
- Cybersecurity Infrastructure Lead

**Decision Rights:** Operational decisions including CAPEX expenditures below $100,000 per location, vendor selection within budget parameters, staff scheduling and training programs, safety system implementation timelines, and cybersecurity protocol execution

**Decision Mechanism:** Consensus-based decision making among core team members. Venue Operations Managers have autonomy for location-specific operational decisions within PMO guidelines.

**Meeting Cadence:** Weekly during project launch phase (first 12 months), bi-weekly thereafter once operations stabilize

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Location-by-location progress updates on warehouse modifications and HVAC compliance
- Hardware procurement status for drones and custom collision sensors
- Staff certification training completion rates across all locations
- Cybersecurity penetration testing schedule and encryption implementation status
- Incident report review from prior week with corrective action plans

**Escalation Path:** Issues exceeding PMO authority on operational decisions escalate to PSC. Safety incidents requiring immediate regulatory notification escalate directly to Safety & Compliance Oversight Board.
### 3. Safety & Compliance Oversight Board

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Critical dedicated body for managing high-liability risks inherent in drone operations with human players. Addresses FAA Part 107 compliance, insurance viability concerns, cybersecurity data protection (GDPR-like protocols), and community safety perception issues that could result in regulatory shutdown or legal liability.

**Responsibilities:**

- Oversee all regulatory compliance including FAA Part 107 licensing across three locations
- Review and approve binding insurance quotes from specialized drone liability carriers
- Validate cybersecurity penetration testing results before public launch acceptance
- Monitor safety incident rates per 1000 hours of operation
- Manage community town hall engagement addressing noise/safety concerns
- Ensure ballistic netting and emergency stop zone compliance across all venues

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Establish safety incident reporting protocol with mandatory escalation timelines
- Secure independent external aviation consultant for regulatory compliance review
- Create comprehensive risk register specific to drone operations and liability exposure
- Define clear thresholds for operational suspension based on safety metrics

**Membership:**

- Safety Compliance Coordinator (Chair)
- External Aviation Consultant - Independent FAA Part 107 Expert
- Cybersecurity Compliance Auditor - External Third Party
- Insurance Broker Specializing in Drone Liability
- Venue Safety Officers from each location

**Decision Rights:** Authority to suspend operations at any location if safety metrics exceed thresholds, approve operational modifications related to safety systems, validate cybersecurity compliance before launch acceptance, and make decisions on insurance coverage adequacy for high-speed drone liability risks

**Decision Mechanism:** Consensus-based decision making. External aviation consultant has veto authority on regulatory compliance matters requiring FAA approval. Safety suspension decisions require unanimous vote among board members.

**Meeting Cadence:** Bi-weekly during project launch phase (first 12 months), monthly thereafter once operations stabilize

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- FAA Part 107 licensing status updates across all three locations with waiver risk analysis
- Insurance premium contingency fund tracking against $50,000 annual budget
- Cybersecurity penetration testing validation results for telemetry data encryption
- Safety incident rate review with corrective action plans
- Community town hall feedback regarding noise/safety concerns

**Escalation Path:** Issues requiring immediate regulatory notification escalate directly to PSC. Safety suspension decisions by board are immediately binding and require PSC acknowledgment within 24 hours.
### 4. Technology & Innovation Advisory Group

**Rationale for Inclusion:** Necessary specialized body for managing technology integration risks including custom collision sensors, real-time player tracking telemetry, AI-driven mission systems, and cybersecurity infrastructure. Addresses critical trade-off between user experience fidelity versus hardware maintenance costs while ensuring data protection compliance.

**Responsibilities:**

- Review technology strategy decisions balancing user experience quality against hardware maintenance costs
- Validate cybersecurity measures for connected player tracking systems including AES-256 encryption standards
- Oversee custom collision sensor integration and real-time telemetry latency thresholds
- Assess AI-driven mission system implementation feasibility and scalability
- Review supply chain resilience for critical drone components and sensors
- Monitor novelty fatigue metrics affecting customer retention rates

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Establish technology risk assessment framework with defined latency thresholds
- Engage external cybersecurity firm for penetration testing schedule
- Create technology vendor evaluation criteria based on safety certification standards
- Define data protection protocols aligned with GDPR-like compliance requirements

**Membership:**

- Chief Technology Officer (Chair)
- External Cybersecurity Auditor - Independent Third Party
- Drone Hardware Supplier Representative
- Software Development Lead for telemetry systems
- Data Privacy Compliance Specialist

**Decision Rights:** Authority to approve technology integration decisions including custom sensor procurement, cybersecurity infrastructure implementation timelines, AI system deployment feasibility, and data protection protocol validation. Can recommend technology suspension if latency thresholds exceed safety limits.

**Decision Mechanism:** Majority voting with external cybersecurity auditor having veto authority on data protection matters requiring regulatory compliance. Technology suspension recommendations require unanimous vote among board members.

**Meeting Cadence:** Monthly during project launch phase (first 12 months), quarterly thereafter once operations stabilize

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Custom collision sensor integration progress and latency performance metrics
- Cybersecurity penetration testing schedule and encryption implementation status
- Data protection compliance review for player telemetry systems
- Technology vendor evaluation for supply chain resilience assessment
- Novelty fatigue analysis affecting customer retention rates

**Escalation Path:** Issues requiring technology suspension escalate to Safety & Compliance Oversight Board. Technology strategy decisions exceeding advisory group authority escalate to PSC.
### 5. Ethics & Community Relations Committee

Manages veteran group partnerships with legal boundaries to avoid active military conflict endorsement while maintaining market authenticity appeal.

**Responsibilities:**

- Review narrative branding strategy balancing military themes with commercial safety perceptions
- Manage veteran group partnership agreements ensuring legal boundaries compliance
- Oversee community town hall engagement addressing noise and safety concerns
- Monitor public perception risks regarding youth participation restrictions
- Validate marketing materials for regulatory compliance regarding combat simulation terminology
- Review dual-branding approach (tactical sport vs combat simulation) effectiveness

**Initial Setup Actions:**

- Establish community engagement protocol with mandatory town hall schedule
- Create legal review process for veteran group partnership agreements
- Define marketing material approval workflow including regulatory compliance checks
- Develop youth participation policy with parental consent requirements

**Membership:**

- Marketing Director (Chair)
- External Community Relations Advisor - Independent Third Party
- Legal Counsel Specializing in Entertainment Regulations
- Veteran Group Partnership Coordinator
- Customer Experience Lead

**Decision Rights:** Authority to approve narrative branding materials before public release, validate veteran group partnership agreements for legal compliance, decide on marketing pivot strategies based on community feedback, and authorize dual-branding approach modifications. Can recommend full brand repositioning if perception risks exceed thresholds.

**Decision Mechanism:** Consensus-based decision making with external community relations advisor having veto authority on public-facing materials requiring regulatory approval. Brand repositioning recommendations require unanimous vote among board members.

**Meeting Cadence:** Monthly during project launch phase (first 12 months), quarterly thereafter once operations stabilize

**Typical Agenda Items:**

- Community town hall feedback analysis regarding noise and safety concerns
- Veteran group partnership agreement legal review for active military conflict endorsement risks
- Marketing material approval workflow including combat simulation terminology compliance
- Youth participation policy effectiveness monitoring with parental consent tracking
- Public perception sentiment scores from customer acquisition channels

**Escalation Path:** Issues requiring regulatory notification on marketing materials escalate to Safety & Compliance Oversight Board. Brand repositioning decisions exceeding committee authority escalate to PSC.
